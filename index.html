House for sport, fitness & recovery in Japan

We place the right object
in the right hands.

PITCHBRIDGE is not a catalogue importer. We represent a short list of performance and wellness houses to people in Japan who already train — professionals, academies, clubs, schools, and studios.

01

Access

The work began in football: English for professional players, then the youth pathway. The same rooms now open to fitness, recovery, and health.

Professionals
Daily proximity through coaching. The people who decide what enters a bag.
Pathway
Academies, clubs, and schools — coaches, parents, the next decade of demand.
Studios
Fitness and recovery rooms. High-performers who are not on a team sheet.
Market
Six and a half years at Recruit Holdings. Strategy, not guesswork.
02

Mandate

We take less, so that what we take can be believed.

We take

  • Sport technology that changes how an athlete trains or recovers
  • Wellness and health objects with a precise point of view
  • Brands prepared for a considered Japan entry — exclusive or not

We leave

  • Generic catalogues and opportunistic SKUs
  • Translated brochures without a Japan thesis
  • Partners who need a warehouse more than a channel
03

Method

  1. Read

    The product, the claims, the culture. Whether Japan will hold it or drop it.

  2. Name

    Language, packaging, compliance. Nothing that sounds imported.

  3. Introduce

    B2C to athletes and families. B2B to clubs, schools, and facilities.

  4. Hold

    Distribution rights, honest feedback, a plan a board can read.
