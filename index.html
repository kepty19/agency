The work

One team holds the Japan operation.

Japan has its own culture, customs, and language. The same people who sell also run the campaign, the support, and the review.

You hand over

  • The first call through to delivery
  • Support before the purchase and after it
  • The Japan operation, as one piece of work

You receive

  • Numbers and progress, shared each day in English when you want them
  • A weekly meeting for the review and the next actions
  • One counterpart for the whole operation

06

Questions

Does Kepty help overseas companies enter the Japan market?
Yes. Kepty Co., Ltd. is the Japan-side partner. It runs sales, marketing, customer support, and analysis as one operation, and reports to companies outside Japan in English.
What does Kepty take on for a Japan entry?
Sales (outbound calls, meetings, and orders), marketing (web, social, and exhibitions in Japan), customer support before and after purchase, and analysis with the next actions. The work is not tied to one product category.
What does headquarters receive?
Numbers and progress can be shared each day, in English. A weekly meeting is also available for the review and the next actions.
Is the work limited to one industry?
No. The operation is the same across product categories. The first conversation names the category, the buyer, and the quarter.
How can an overseas company contact Kepty?
Email contact@kepty.co or use the inquiry form on this page. We reply once we have reviewed it.
